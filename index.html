Two modes, one band

Switch between keeping an eye on your body and keeping your study routine healthy.

Health mode

Builds a personal baseline from your heart rate, SpO₂ and motion. It flags persistent unusual patterns, not one noisy reading, and shows an experimental anomaly score.

Anomaly detection only. Not a medical diagnosis.

Student mode

Tracks study sessions, long sitting stretches, movement and breaks. When you have been still too long, the band buzzes and the OLED suggests a short walk.

AI study insights

Python and scikit-learn group similar study habits, compare you with an anonymous cohort and write recommendations in plain language.

      How it works

      1. Sense

        MAX30100 reads heart rate and SpO₂. The motion sensor tracks movement and wrist position.

      2. Send

        The ESP32-S3 sends a reading over Wi-Fi to a ThingSpeak channel every 20 seconds.

      3. Show

        This site reads the channel and draws live values, graphs and history in your browser.

      4. Learn

        Exported data is analysed in Jupyter to find study patterns and write recommendations.

      What is inside

      ESP32-S3

      Dual-core microcontroller with Wi-Fi. Runs the sensing, scoring and upload.

      MAX30100

      Pulse oximeter for heart rate and SpO₂.

      Motion sensor

      Accelerometer and gyroscope for activity, orientation and stillness.

      OLED and buzzer

      Instant feedback on the wrist: readings, mode and break reminders.

      HC-SR501 (optional)

      Detects presence at the study desk. Not sent to the cloud in this build.
